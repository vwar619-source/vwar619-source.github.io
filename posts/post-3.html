RTI Application for ICAI Answer Sheet Copies

CA Updates • 22 June 2026

After the declaration of examination results, many students are often left wondering where they lost marks and how they can improve their performance in future attempts. Obtaining a copy of the evaluated answer sheet can provide valuable insights into the assessment process, helping students understand their mistakes, identify areas for improvement, and develop a more effective examination strategy. Through the RTI Act, students can access their answer sheets and use them as a practical learning tool for achieving better results in subsequent examinations.

Why Should Students Obtain Answer Sheet Copies Through RTI?

Obtaining a copy of the evaluated answer sheet can be highly beneficial for students appearing for future examinations.

  • •Understand examiner expectations and the standard of answers required.
  • •Identify mistakes that led to loss of marks.
  • •Improve answer-writing techniques and presentation skills.
  • •Recognize weak areas requiring additional preparation.
  • •Avoid repeating the same errors in future attempts.
  • •Assess whether revaluation is necessary before spending time and money on review procedures.
  • •Develop a focused study strategy based on actual performance.
  • •Gain transparency and confidence in the evaluation process.

Rather than viewing the answer sheet as a record of past results, students can use it as a valuable learning tool to improve their performance in subsequent attempts.

Step-by-Step Procedure

The students are advised to follow the steps and file the application during the period between 10:00 P.M. and 7:00 A.M. for better and smoother filing experience.

Step 1 – Go to the RTI Online Portal

Visit the RTI Online Portal and click on Submit Request. If you are a first-time user, create an account using your email address.

Step 2 – Select ICAI as the Public Authority

Search and select Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) from the list of public authorities.

Step 3 – Fill Personal Details Carefully

Use the same Name, Email Address and Mobile Number as available in ICAI records to avoid mismatches and delays.

Step 4 – Paste the RTI Application Template

Paste the RTI application and fill in your personal details. Only alphabets A-Z a-z number 0-9 and special characters, . - _ ( ) / @ : & ? \ % are allowed in Text for RTI Request application. 

        Subject: Request for Certified Copies of Evaluated Answer Books under RTI Act, 2005

        Dear Sir / Madam,

        I hereby request certified copies of my evaluated answer books under the Right to Information Act, 2005. My examination details are as follows:

        1. Full Name: [Your Full Name as per ICAI records]
        2. Date of Birth: [DD/MM/YYYY]
        3. Registration Number: [Your ICAI Registration Number]
        4. Roll Number: [Your Exam Roll Number]
        5. Examination: [CA Foundation / CA Intermediate / CA Final]
        6. Session: January 2026
        7. Contact Number: [Your Mobile Number]

        I request certified copies of my evaluated answer books for the following papers:

        1. Paper 01 – Financial Reporting
        2. Paper 02 – Advanced Financial Management
        3. Paper 03 – Advanced Auditing, Assurance & Professional Ethics
        4. Paper 04 – Direct Tax Laws and International Taxation
        5. Paper 05 – Indirect Tax Laws
        6. Paper 06 – Integrated Business Solutions

        I have attached a self-attested copy of my Admit Card as proof of identity.

        Yours sincerely,

        [Your Full Name]
        [Date]

Important: Only alphabets (A-Z, a-z), numbers (0-9) and special characters . - _ ( ) / @ : & ? \ % are permitted in the RTI request text.

Step 5 – Upload Self-Attested Admit Card

Upload a clear scan or photograph of your self-attested admit card along with your signature.

Step 6 – Pay ₹10 Online

Pay the prescribed RTI fee of ₹10. This single fee covers all papers requested in the application.

What Happens After Submission?

After successful filing, you will receive an RTI reference number. The certified answer sheet copies are generally sent to your registered email address within 15–20 days.

Conclusion

For students, obtaining a copy of the evaluated answer sheet is more than just reviewing past performance—it is an opportunity to learn from mistakes and improve future results. By understanding how answers were assessed, identifying areas for improvement, and refining examination strategies, students can approach their next attempt with greater clarity and confidence. A small effort today in seeking transparency can lead to better preparation and improved scores in future examinations.
